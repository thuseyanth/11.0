About BOSS INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS

Your Trusted Partner in Industrial Solutions — At BOSS INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS, we specialize in industrial water and wastewater treatment — offering both advanced chemical solutions and complete project support. With a team that brings over 50 years of combined experience, we bridge the gap between chemical expertise and engineering know-how. Many of our members have spent years working hands-on with water treatment processes, giving us deep insight into real-world challenges across industries. We supply a broad range of water treatment chemicals suitable for all industrial applications. More than just products, we provide full-service support tailored to each customer’s needs — helping improve process efficiency, performance, and long-term reliability.
